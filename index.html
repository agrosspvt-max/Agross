Balanced Farming

Balanced farming , ‘Santulit kheti’ in Hindi, is an integration of chemical as well organic & biological agriinputs in most practical & environmentally friendly way.The perfect balance and preservation of these factors mean that farming practices are socially responsible, environmentally sustainable, and economically viable, promoting long-term sustainability and resilience, not only for people, but also for the planet.
