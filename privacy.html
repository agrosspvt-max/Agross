1. Information we collect and why

Information you share when contacting us

When you submit an enquiry form, we receive the details you enter and use them to handle your request. We send the submission to our business inbox through Brevo and use the email address you provide to reply.

General contact
Your name, email address and message, plus your phone number or website if you provide them. We use these details to respond to your enquiry.
Dealership enquiry
Your name, firm name, mobile number, email address, state, district, tehsil, dealership type and remarks. We use these details to consider your dealership enquiry and contact you about it.
Contract or custom farming enquiry
Your company name, email address, mobile number, authorised person’s name, produce, approximate quantity and address. We use these details to assess your request and follow up about potential arrangements.
Recruitment enquiry
Your name, contact number, email address, profile or role of interest, state and address, plus your resume if you attach one. We use these details to consider your application and contact you about recruitment. The resume is stored with your submission and included in the email to our business inbox.

If you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, we receive the contact details, messages and other information you choose to share. We use them to respond and keep track of the related correspondence. Please provide another person’s information only if you are authorised to share it.

You can browse the catalogue without contacting us. If your message leaves out details we need to handle your enquiry, we may ask you for them.

Information from website visits

When your browser requests a page, the hosting service receives technical information such as your IP address, the requested page, and browser or device information associated with the request. This information may be used to deliver and protect the website, diagnose errors, and understand how the site performs. The services listed in section 3 may receive technical information when they load.

We use personal information for the purposes described here and for related administration, security and legal obligations. Where consent is required for a use, we ask for it for that purpose. You can contact us about withdrawing consent as described in section 6.

2. Who receives information

  • Agromaxx Industry. Information is used by the people handling your enquiry, dealership request, farming enquiry or recruitment enquiry.
  • Email service providers. Brevo processes form details to deliver enquiries to info@agromaxx.in. If you email us directly, your email provider and ours handle delivery, storage and replies. See the Brevo Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel, our website host. Vercel receives technical information when it delivers website pages and runs the function that processes form submissions. See the Vercel Privacy Notice.
  • External services. The providers described in section 3 may receive information when their features load or you use their services.
  • Authorities and other recipients where required by law. We may disclose information when required by law, a court order or a lawful request from a competent authority, or where otherwise permitted by law to protect rights or address security incidents.

3. Cookies and external services

Some pages load resources from other providers. Your browser may contact those providers when the resource loads, even if you have not clicked it. Providers may receive your IP address and browser information, and may use cookies or similar browser storage according to their services and your settings.

  • Google Translate, Fonts and Maps. We use Google services for language translation, typefaces and the map on our contact page. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Other page resources. Some pages request styles, icons or scripts from content delivery services to display the site and support its features.
  • WhatsApp and other external links. If you follow a link to WhatsApp, a social network or another website, that service handles your activity under its own privacy terms. We receive the information you choose to send us. See the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.

You can manage or delete cookies in your browser settings and block third-party resources if your browser supports it. Blocking resources can affect features such as translation and the map. If you have questions about website tracking or want to withdraw consent for a particular use, please contact us.

4. How long we keep information

How long we keep information depends on the purpose of the record. For enquiries and applications, relevant factors include whether we are still considering or following up on the request, whether we need the record to manage a dispute, and any legal recordkeeping requirements. Retention can differ for correspondence, backups and technical logs.

You can contact us about how long a specific record is kept or to request its deletion. We may retain information where the law requires or permits us to keep it.

5. Security and international processing

The website uses HTTPS to protect form submissions in transit. Please leave passwords, government ID numbers, bank or card details, and medical information out of your messages. Where required by law, we will notify affected people and the relevant authority of a personal data breach.

Our hosting and external service providers may process information outside India. The location depends on the service involved. Where information is processed internationally, applicable data protection requirements apply. You can contact us for more information about the arrangements relevant to your data.

6. Your choices, rights and complaints

Depending on the law that applies to your information, you may be able to:

  • Request access to your personal information or a summary of how we process and share it.
  • Ask us to correct, complete or update inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Ask us to erase information, subject to applicable retention requirements.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing and may affect our ability to continue handling your request.
  • Nominate someone to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity, where that right applies.
  • Raise a complaint about how we handle your information.

To exercise a right, withdraw consent or raise a grievance, email info@agromaxx.in. Tell us which request or correspondence you are referring to and what action you would like. We may ask for enough information to verify your identity or authority. We handle requests within the time limits that apply by law.

Where the applicable law provides a right to complain to the Data Protection Board of India or another competent authority, you may do so after using our grievance process if that step is required.

7. Children’s privacy

The website is intended for visitors seeking agricultural information, business opportunities or employment. Please do not submit a child’s personal information unless you are authorised to do so. If you believe a child’s information was submitted without the consent required by law, contact us so we can review it and take appropriate action.
